Folio Reader

Read, share and print PDFs.

Download for Android Open in the browser
  1. Already have Folio Reader? Uninstall it first: press and hold its icon, then tap Uninstall.
  2. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns you about the file, tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the download and tap Install. If asked, allow Chrome to install apps.
  4. Open any PDF, choose Folio Reader, then tap Always.
